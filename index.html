001 / A PLACE BECOMES A UNIVERSENEW YORK ROOTS. NO FIXED EDGES.

First Street.
First Avenue.
Infinite possibility.

It began with takeout.
It became a place to be.

Born in New York City’s East Village, Pinky’s Space grew from a restaurant into an artistic environment, a garden, and a gathering place.

Bright pink lights stayed on around the clock. Artists, strangers, neighbors, travelers, and wonderfully unusual characters came to paint, draw, talk, create, think, and simply exist.

The physical space has changed. The community, identity, and creative mission continue.
